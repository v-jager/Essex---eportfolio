Team Project: Airbnb Price Prediction

This project analysed Airbnb listing data in New York City to identify the key drivers of pricing and to develop predictive models for estimating nightly rates. The modelling approach combined traditional statistical techniques with machine learning methods, allowing both interpretability and improved predictive performance.

My contribution focused on the modelling framework and the interpretation of results. We developed and evaluated both a linear regression baseline and a random forest regression model to capture non-linear relationships and feature interactions. The comparison showed that while both models achieved similar predictive performance, they provided complementary insights: linear regression offered transparency in understanding feature effects, while random forest highlighted complex interactions and feature importance :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}.

In addition, I contributed to the interpretation of the results from a business perspective, demonstrating how machine learning–based price prediction can support decision-making. These include providing pricing guidance to hosts, improving demand forecasting, and identifying key market drivers such as location, room type, and availability.
