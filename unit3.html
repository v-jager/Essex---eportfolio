Unit 3 Reflection: Correlation and Regression

This activity explored key statistical concepts underlying machine learning models, including covariance, correlation, and different forms of regression. The results highlight that high Pearson correlation does not necessarily imply a linear relationship, as demonstrated by a polynomial example where correlation remained high despite non-linearity. The exercise also compared different regression approaches, including ordinary least squares and more robust alternatives, and showed how model performance can be improved through feature engineering such as one-hot encoding. Finally, polynomial regression illustrated the trade-off between model complexity and overfitting.
