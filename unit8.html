Unit 8: Language-generating AIs and Gradient Descent

This unit combines a critical discussion of large language models with a practical exploration of optimisation techniques in machine learning. The discussion examines the capabilities and limitations of modern AI writing systems, highlighting that despite their fluency, they operate based on statistical pattern recognition rather than true understanding. It also reflects on recent advancements, including reasoning-oriented models, while noting that fundamental limitations such as bias and lack of semantic understanding remain relevant.

The second part of the activity focuses on gradient descent as a core optimisation algorithm. By implementing the method from scratch and experimenting with different parameter settings, the sensitivity of the learning process becomes clear. Small changes in the learning rate lead to significantly different outcomes: while a slightly lower learning rate results in stable convergence, a marginally higher value causes the model to diverge rapidly, with parameters and cost values exploding.

This demonstrates that training machine learning models is fundamentally an optimisation problem, where careful parameter tuning is essential for achieving stable and accurate results.
