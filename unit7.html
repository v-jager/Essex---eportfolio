Unit 7 Reflection: Multi-Layer Perceptron

This activity explored the fundamental principles of feedforward neural networks through the implementation of a multi-layer perceptron (MLP) from scratch. The model was applied to the XOR problem, a classical example demonstrating the limitations of single-layer perceptrons and the necessity of non-linear architectures.

The notebooks provided a detailed walkthrough of the key components of neural networks, including activation functions, forward propagation, error calculation, and backpropagation. The training process illustrated how weights are iteratively updated to minimise prediction error, and the decreasing error over epochs confirmed successful learning.

Overall, this exercise highlights how relatively simple computational elements can be combined to form powerful models, and provides a strong conceptual foundation for understanding more advanced neural network architectures.
