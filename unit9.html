Unit 9 Reflection: CNNs and Image Recognition

This unit explored convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and their application in image recognition tasks. Beyond the technical implementation, it highlighted important real-world implications, particularly through examples of facial recognition systems and their associated challenges such as accuracy, bias, and privacy concerns.

The practical component demonstrated a full CNN workflow using the CIFAR-10 dataset, including preprocessing, model construction, training, and evaluation. Performance was assessed using accuracy metrics and confusion matrices.
